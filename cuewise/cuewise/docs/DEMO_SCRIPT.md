# Cuewise demo script — target 90 to 110 seconds

## 0:00–0:08 — The problem
On screen: Cuewise home.

Voiceover: "The hardest conversations are usually the ones we never practice. Cuewise gives students and early-career people a private rehearsal room before the moment arrives."

## 0:08–0:22 — Choose a moment
Tap `Choose a moment`, then open `Ask for help without sounding unprepared`.

Voiceover: "I choose a situation I actually face: asking for help while still sounding capable."

## 0:22–0:45 — Rehearse
Tap `Start rehearsal`. Type a natural answer. Submit.

Voiceover: "Cuewise gives me a real prompt and a small writing space. It does not ask me to read a lecture; it asks me to say the thing."

## 0:45–1:05 — Feedback
Show the score and the four coaching dimensions. Tap `Try one small upgrade` and revise.

Voiceover: "The feedback is specific: what was clear, what created trust, and one upgrade I can actually use. Then I try again."

## 1:05–1:20 — Progress
Return home and show the progress card.

Voiceover: "The product loop is practice, feedback, second attempt. Progress is measured in reps, not anxiety."

## 1:20–1:35 — Monetization
Open premium scenario or paywall. Show RevenueCat Test Store purchase flow or configured premium entitlement.

Voiceover: "Starter scenarios stay free. The premium pack unlocks deeper situations through a transparent RevenueCat entitlement, so monetization supports repetition instead of interrupting the first win."

## 1:35–1:45 — Close
Show the Cuewise logo and repository URL in the video description.

Voiceover: "Cuewise: practice the conversation before it counts."

## Recording checklist

- Record on a real Android device if possible.
- Keep the app and taps visible; avoid long loading screens.
- Do not show personal email, API keys, or private dashboards.
- Use only original visuals and royalty-free or no music.
- Keep final video under two minutes.
