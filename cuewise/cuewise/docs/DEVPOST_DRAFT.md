# Cuewise — Devpost draft

## Project name
Cuewise

## Elevator pitch
Practice high-stakes conversations with realistic prompts, instant coaching, and a second chance before you say it for real.

## One-line positioning
A calm rehearsal room for the conversations you keep replaying in your head.

## What it does
Cuewise helps students and early-career people prepare for moments such as interviews, asking for help, setting a boundary, disagreeing in a group project, or requesting an opportunity. A user chooses a scenario, writes a response, receives concise coaching, and retries with one targeted improvement.

## Why it matters
Most communication advice is passive: articles, videos, and lists. Cuewise turns advice into practice. The user leaves with a sentence they can actually say, not just a concept they understand.

## Core experience
1. A focused home screen surfaces the next useful rehearsal.
2. Scenario cards make the first step concrete.
3. The rehearsal screen gives a real prompt and a private writing space.
4. The feedback screen scores clarity, empathy, boundaries, and next steps.
5. A second attempt turns feedback into skill.

## RevenueCat integration
Cuewise uses RevenueCat to gate the premium scenario library behind a transparent premium entitlement. The free experience includes starter scenarios; the premium pack adds more difficult, high-value situations. The project is configured for RevenueCat Test Store during development and will use the appropriate platform configuration for any store release.

## What judges should notice
- The app has one sharp job: help someone say the next hard thing.
- Feedback is actionable and kind, not a generic AI paragraph.
- The second-attempt loop makes improvement visible.
- The product is monetized without blocking the first moment of value.
- The visual language uses a quiet midnight palette, warm coral emphasis, and generous spacing to make a stressful task feel safe.
