## Store long description
Cuewise is a calm rehearsal room for the conversations you keep replaying in your head. Choose a realistic situation, write what you would say, get concise coaching, and try again with one small upgrade.

Built for interviews, group projects, asking for help, setting boundaries, and early-career moments where clarity matters.

Cuewise is not a replacement for a manager, counsellor, or professional adviser. It is a private practice tool for finding your next clear sentence.
